# Badge

Small status tag that always includes text. Tones: income (settled, paid), pending (expected, settles soon, waiting to sync), danger (overdue, failed), investment, and neutral (paused, personal, shared).

## Example

```tsx
<Badge tone="income" icon="checkmark">Settled</Badge>
<Badge tone="pending">Settles Nov 30</Badge>
```
